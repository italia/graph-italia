# Projects and organizations

## Projects

Everything you create belongs to a **project**. The selector next to the Private Area title shows the active project and lets you switch it, create new ones or rename them. Each project has its own charts, dashboards, datasets and API keys.

## Organizations

Organizations let several people work together on the same projects. They are managed from **user menu → Organizations**.

- Whoever creates an organization is its **administrator** (ADMIN role);
- administrators invite members by email, and can change roles or remove members;
- members with the **USER** role access the associated projects without administration rights.

## Associating projects

You can move a project from personal to part of an organization. To do so, go to the **Projects** tab of the organization and click **Transfer Project**, then associate one of your personal projects with the organization: all members can see it and work on it. This is not a transfer of ownership:

- you remain the owner of the project;
- you can **revoke** the association at any time; when revoked, the project goes back to being personal.

## Project visibility in the organization

- Members of the organization can see and edit the items of the associated projects;
- the public/private visibility of the items stays independent: private means "only those who access the project", not "only me".
