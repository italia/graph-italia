# Introduction

Graph Italia is the platform that turns your data into charts, maps, numeric indicators (KPIs) and dashboards. It is designed to make public administration data easier to visualise and works with any tabular dataset.

## How it is organised

- **Personal projects**: this is where you find the list of everything you have created. Every account already has a personal project; you can create more and share them with other people through the [Organizations](/docs/organizzazioni) feature.
- **Items**: items are the materials you have created: charts, KPI groups, maps, dashboards and data sources. They are listed in your Private Area.
- **Visibility**: each item can be set to **public** (visible to anyone with the link and embeddable in other sites) or **private** (visible only inside your Private Area). You can change the visibility of your items in the **Setup Info** section of the editor.

## First steps

1. From your **Private Area**, click **Create new** and choose the type of item you want to create.
2. Load the data from a local file (CSV, JSON) or from an external URL. Check that the data was imported correctly, or fix it.
3. Configure the look of the item using the live preview.
4. Save, publish and share the link or the embed code.

Read the [Getting started](/quickstart) guide for practical tips on using the platform. The chapters of this documentation, instead, go deeper into each part.
