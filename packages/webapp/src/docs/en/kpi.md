# KPI groups

A KPI group is a summary of the numeric data in the chart.

## When to use it

To make reading easier and show numeric data concisely, at a glance.

## How to create a KPI group

1. Go to **Create new**, select **KPI Group** and give it a title.
2. In the editor, add one KPI at a time with the **Add KPI** form.

## The fields of a KPI

- **Title**: what the indicator measures (required);
- **Value**: the main number or text, highlighted (required);
- Value **prefix** and **suffix**: for units of measure and symbols (€, %, M, k);
- **Flow** (optional, enabled with the **Show flow** switch): positive or negative direction, the amount of the change and a text detail (for example "compared to 2024");
- **Percentage**: a share to show next to the value;
- **Footer text**: the source or a note.

## Configuration

In the **Configuration** section, choose the layout of the tiles: **horizontal** (side by side, recommended in dashboards) or **vertical** (stacked). The preview on the right updates as you go; in that section you edit or delete the individual KPIs.

## How to add it to dashboards

A KPI group goes into a dashboard like any chart: a full-width slot with the horizontal layout is the most effective format.
